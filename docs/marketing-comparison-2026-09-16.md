# BrostCo and GovDash: republished landing-page review

Reviewed September 16, 2026. Sources: [BrostCo](https://brostco.com/) and [GovDash](https://www.govdash.com/).

## Verdict

GovDash currently gives the stronger impression of an established enterprise software company. Its coordinated product motion, customer evidence, and concise content modules reinforce each other. BrostCo explains the everyday work more plainly and offers a more direct, transparent trial path. BrostCo should borrow the discipline of GovDash's presentation, not its jargon or sales-led conversion model.

The republished BrostCo page is substantially clearer than the older screenshots, but not yet best-in-class. Its biggest remaining weaknesses are uneven vertical rhythm, long example panels, insufficiently persistent mobile exploration controls, older product recordings, and limited independent customer evidence. Attractive styling alone cannot close the last two gaps.

## Scope and evidence limits

The comparison evaluates the two public landing pages, their section structure, visible navigation, copy, product presentations, and directly inspected desktop interactions. GovDash gallery selection and BrostCo workflow/source interactions were exercised. BrostCo's live page was confirmed to contain the previous revision's headline and contrast fixes before this revision began.

Live browser viewport was 1363 × 936. The supplied mobile screenshots provide additional visual context but are not proof of the latest responsive state. Revised BrostCo layouts are covered separately by repository browser regression tests at desktop, tablet, mobile, narrow-mobile, and landscape sizes. GovDash's current tablet/mobile interactions were not independently tested in this pass. No completed signup, purchase, authenticated GovDash workflow, real AI output, or security certification was independently validated. This is a landing-page comparison, not certification of either entire product.

## Changes implemented in this revision

### Critical

- Hero agency: “AI finds government contracts and prepares your bids.” Supporting copy covers scoring, optional outreach, quote capture, assembly, rules, calls, and final review. This fixes the implication that the visitor performs discovery.
- Mobile/tablet workflow navigation: section-bound sticky tabs, short readable mobile labels, previous/next controls, keyboard navigation, and repositioning after a stage change. This fixes scroll-back effort and prevents landing halfway through the next preview.
- Contextual trial entry: the existing sticky header exposes its trial button on mobile once the hero leaves view. No extra bottom banner covers content or competes with the step controls.

### High priority

- Fluid section spacing replaces inconsistent fixed breakpoint spacing. Heading/link groups use explicit positive gaps rather than negative-margin compensation. Tablet workflow columns stack for readable previews.
- Source lightbox uses a native modal, named heading, close control, Escape dismissal, contained scrolling, background scroll lock, and focus restoration. It explains what is extracted and what still needs verification without stretching the preview.
- First-week plan replaces generic setup copy with concrete trial checkpoints and honest dependencies. Outreach is optional, not an unavoidable prerequisite.
- Hero overlay is lighter. The standalone pause button is removed and automatic motion is limited to a short introduction, then a still frame. Reduced-motion and data-saving preferences retain the poster.

### Enhancements

- Scrolling industry ribbon supplies quiet visual rhythm using the actual discovery-sector catalog and consistent icons. It is explicitly industry coverage, not implied customer endorsement.
- Ribbon motion has a pause control, pauses on hover/focus and offscreen, and disables animation for reduced motion. The existing full industry directory remains the accessible exploration destination.

## Next improvements that require evidence, not more decoration

1. Record the current workspace. Show one real, permissioned pursuit from discovered opportunity to requirement brief, outreach, captured quotes, assembled draft, and human review. Keep sensitive data out of the recording.
2. Publish genuine customer evidence. One specific, attributable case study is more persuasive than a large unsupported logo wall.
3. Measure the funnel before claiming a conversion lift. Track hero trial clicks, workflow engagement, pricing exploration, signup completion, connection completion, and first useful result. Avoid collecting document or email contents in marketing analytics.
4. Validate trial activation with new users. Ask them to find their first match and explain what will happen automatically before enabling outreach. Use observed confusion to improve defaults and setup text.
5. Keep essential information visible. Reserve lightboxes for source evidence and expanded previews, not pricing, limitations, trial conditions, or the primary value proposition.

## Readiness boundary

Passing builds, unit tests, and responsive browser regressions supports shipping this marketing revision. It does not establish enterprise security certification, guarantee compliant AI output, verify live provider behavior, prove a conversion lift, or substitute for customer evidence. Merge and publication are separate steps: a successful merge must still be deployed and checked on the live domain.
