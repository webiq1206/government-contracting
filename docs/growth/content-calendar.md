## 1. Bid decision worksheet

Before you spend a day preparing a bid, answer three questions:

Can we meet the requirements? Can we hit every mandatory date? Can we actually deliver the work?

If any answer is unclear, that's the next task. Writing a longer proposal won't resolve it.

We built a free BrostCo bid/no-bid worksheet to make those checks easier to discuss with your team. Seven checks, a downloadable record, and no signup.

## 3. Proposal requirements

A proposal can read well and still miss an instruction.

For each requirement, record its source, who owns the response, where the response appears, and whether your team has checked it.

## 4. Construction source guide

For a construction opportunity, the deadline on the cover page is only part of the schedule.

Check site visits, the question cutoff, subcontractor quote dates and final review. Keep the drawing revision and latest amendment beside each trade's scope.
