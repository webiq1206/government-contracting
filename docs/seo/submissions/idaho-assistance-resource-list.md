# Submission: Idaho business-assistance resource lists

**Destination link:** `https://brostco.com/resources/idaho-procurement-sources`
**Status:** prepared, not submitted
**Account needed:** none
**Fee:** none

Targets: Idaho APEX Accelerator, Idaho SBDC, Idaho Commerce, Boise Metro
Chamber, and county or city economic development offices that publish a
resources page for local businesses.

## Why this placement is legitimate

These organizations counsel Idaho businesses on the exact problem the register
solves, and several already publish a page of links for that audience. Three
things make this a contribution rather than a request:

- The register links out to the Idaho APEX Accelerator's no-cost counselling on
  its own initiative, unreciprocated, and says plainly that the accelerator is
  not a bid board and cannot answer a question on a buyer's behalf. That is
  accurate and useful to them.
- It sends readers to each authority's own page rather than to a commercial
  aggregator, which is what these organizations tell clients to do anyway.
- It is free, ungated, and correctable by anyone.

## Approach

Use the organization's public resource-suggestion route or general contact form.
Do not build an email list and do not send a sequence. One submission through a
public channel, then leave it.

A counsellor's own bookmark is worth more than a link, so the framing should be
"this may save your counsellors some time", not "please link to us". If they use
it internally and never publish a link, that is a fine outcome and the submission
was still worth making.

## Submission text

> Subject: A free Idaho procurement source register, for your resources page
>
> We maintain a free reference that may be useful to the businesses you counsel:
>
> **Idaho public procurement sources, by buying authority**
> https://brostco.com/resources/idaho-procurement-sources
>
> It exists because of one recurring problem. Idaho has no single public bid
> board, so a vendor who registers with one purchasing authority reasonably
> believes they are now seeing Idaho's public work. State purchasing does not
> advertise state facility construction; neither advertises City of Boise work;
> none of them cover Ada County Highway District roadway work, including the
> streets inside Boise; and federal work performed in Idaho appears on none of
> them at all.
>
> For each of seven authorities the register gives the authority's own official
> source, what is advertised there, what registration is required to receive
> notices, and what checking that source still leaves out. The last field is the
> one we could not find published anywhere else, and it is the one that changes a
> vendor's behaviour.
>
> It is free and ungated, with no account or email capture. The data is also
> published as JSON and CSV so you can reuse the rows on your own pages rather
> than link out, if that suits you better, and it is free to quote with
> attribution. Every entry names the date it was last checked by hand.
>
> Disclosure: we also sell software for government contractors. The register is
> free reference material, it lists the Idaho APEX Accelerator's no-cost
> assistance as an independent resource, and we correct errors regardless of
> whether the reporter is a customer. If an authority is missing or a URL has
> moved, hello@brostco.com and we will fix it.

## What not to do

- Do not ask for a link in exchange for anything.
- Do not submit to more than one contact at the same organization.
- Do not claim endorsement by any authority listed in the register.
- If a chamber offers a paid member directory listing, treat that as a
  membership decision on its merits, not as a backlink purchase. Verify the
  directory's link attribute first; member directories are frequently nofollow.
